Salt Alert ❄️

Check each evening: should you put out salt tonight?

How it decides: regular rock salt stops working well when it's colder than about 10–15 °F. So this app recommends salt only when snow or freezing rain is coming and it'll be warm enough for salt to actually melt it. If it's colder than that, it suggests sand or a cold-weather deicer instead. Forecast data from Open-Meteo (free).
📱 Get automatic phone alerts: install the free ntfy app (App Store or Google Play), tap + Subscribe to topic, and enter salt-alert-mckinley-lwvu74. Every evening at 5 pm this app checks tomorrow's Chicago forecast and sends a notification if it's a salt night.
